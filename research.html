Research

Comparing Reinforcement and Supervised Learning for tuning Large Language-specific Models (2024)
Development of an Italian LLM using a multi-stage fine-tuning and reinforcement learning process, including Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with the aim of improving language proficiency, content, and grammar correctness.
Benchita
High-quality few-shot benchmark suite for evaluating Italian Large Language Models, supporting the assessment of language understanding, generation capabilities, and task-specific performance.
Search Engine Implementation
Implementation from scratch of a search engine based on an inverted-index architecture. The project includes document indexing, query processing, and relevance ranking using BM25 and TF-IDF scoring functions.
SpeakItalian
A computer vision tool for hand gesture recognition, designed to detect and classify gestures and enable more natural human-computer interaction.
Heavy-Tailed Variational Autoencoders
Variational Autoencoder architecture based on Normal, Log-Normal, and Inverse Gaussian distributions. The project explores heavy-tailed latent-variable modelling for learning robust and expressive latent representations.
Movement formation of a swarm of Robots
MATLAB and Simulink implementation of a multi-robot formation-control system. The project simulates a swarm of robots coordinating their movement to maintain a predefined formation while navigating towards a target position.
Anomaly Detection on Embedded Devices (2021)
Deployment of an anomaly-detection model on NVIDIA Jetson Nano and Raspberry Pi devices for identifying anomalous patterns in time-series telemetry data. The project focuses on enabling efficient edge-AI inference in resource-constrained embedded environments. Research project part of HORIZON2020, an European program.
